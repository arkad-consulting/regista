# Talking-head reel

A person speaking to the camera, vertical 9:16.

- The hook lands in the first 1–3 seconds: a promise or a question, with something moving on screen (a punch-in, a title, a change of framing). Never a static talking head at the start.
- Cut on word boundaries and keep the breaths: gaps of 0.2–0.7 s read as natural.
- Alternate the framing (punch-ins) so jump cuts do not show.
- Short captions: two or three words at a time, large, in the caption band.
- Keep captions and titles out of the top ~8 %, the bottom ~20 % and the right edge of the frame, where the platform draws its buttons.
- The face stays clear: eyes in the upper third, no card over it.
- Inserts (B-roll) start on the sentence they illustrate, never in a silence.
- Of the readings of a line, keep the cleanest complete one (no stumble, no restart, a complete ending), usually the last; fillers and false starts go.
