# Screen tutorial

A screen recording made in Regista, with or without the face, 16:9 or 9:16.

- Say at the start what the viewer will be able to do at the end; in a long tutorial the opening is over within 30 seconds.
- Zoom on the action (1.5–2×) with a soft move, and keep the cursor easy to follow.
- Remove pauses longer than about 1.5 s; speed up repetitive steps.
- One step at a time: a callout on the thing the voice names, then the next.
- Text on the screen stays readable after the zoom: small interface text needs a closer zoom, not a smaller caption.
- In 16:9, captions keep to two lines of about 42 characters.
- The logo comes only at the end, after the last spoken word.
