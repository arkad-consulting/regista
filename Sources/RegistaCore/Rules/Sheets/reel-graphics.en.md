# Graphics on a reel

Titles, cards, inserts, sound effects and music on top of a finished edit.

- One hook title in the opening, never two kinds of title at once; no captions while it shows.
- One card at a time, about one every 6–8 seconds, each on screen 2–4 seconds and readable at phone size.
- A title that has landed stays still.
- Cards and titles live in the top band, never over the face or in the caption band.
- A sound effect only where something happens on screen, quiet, never two at once.
- The logo is a closing card only, after the last word and its pause; never a watermark in a corner.
- On a 1080×1920 frame: titles and cards in the top band (y 110–640), the face (about y 420–1180) clear except for a full-frame insert, the caption band (y 1230–1460) always clean.
- Most lines get nothing: the face and the captions carry the reel. A title is the line's point in a few words, a card says what the voice does not, an insert shows what the line names; one layer at a time, never stacked.
- Text behind the person: the hook title only, once per reel.
- Fine, abstract touches: no literal decoration, no gimmicks (stock icons, emoji rain, shaking text); when in doubt, leave it out.
