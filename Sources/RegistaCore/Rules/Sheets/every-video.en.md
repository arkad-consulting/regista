# For every video

These rules hold for every kind of video. The sheet of the project's kind adds its own on top. Before delivery: one round of fixes, then a verdict per check (passed, fixed, known issue, not checked). A sound that was measured is never said to have been listened to. The choices in the header (caption style, pace, framing, music…) are the person's: follow them, and change one only when the person asks.

## Always
- The person's original files are never modified or deleted: the edit works on the project.
- A cloned voice is only ever the person's own.
- A paid generation only after its cost is told and the person approves it.
- Nothing invented on screen: no fact, number, name or quote that was not said or given.
- Look at the frames before delivering, not only at the measurements.
- Build what the person said yes to; one fix asked for is that fix only.

## Opening
- The first 3 seconds say what the video is about and why it is worth staying. Never give away the result or the answer there: keep it for the payoff.
- The first frame is a usable thumbnail: a face, a bold line or the subject, never black and never a logo alone.
- The voice starts right away: no silent build-up, no greeting before the point.
- While the opening title is on screen there are no captions: one thing to read at a time.
- One subject, one goal and one turn (the moment that changes what the viewer thinks): what serves none of the three goes.

## Picture
- The face is never cut or covered: not by a cut, a zoom, a caption, a graphic or a change of format; there is always room above the hair.

## Captions
- One caption group on screen at a time, and each one disappears when its words are done.
- The first caption never sits at exactly 0 s.
- Captions never repeat a text already on screen (a title, a card).
- Captions never cover the speaker's face and stay out of the platform's buttons and bars.
- No caption after the last spoken word: words the transcription invents over a silent end are removed.
- The words of a line sit on one baseline.

## Graphics and text
- Every line stays long enough to be read: 0.6 s + 1 s per 12 letters (6 for Chinese, Japanese, Korean), whole in frame.
- Nothing meant to be read is smaller than 28 px on a 1080-wide frame.
- No logo, title or card over the speaker's face.
- One reading task at a time: never two loud texts in the same frame.
- Colours and fonts come from the brand kit only, in every graphic and scene.

## Ending
- The spoken payoff ends completely before anything closes.
- After the last word, 0.3 to 0.6 s of clean picture, then the logo.
- The logo lands once, at the end, and holds about 1.5 to 3 s. Never at the start, never docked in a corner for the whole video.
- No caption on the closing logo: the last caption clears before it.
- The ending echoes the opening: its picture, its phrase or its sound comes back, changed by what was said.

## Sound
- The voice at −15 LUFS, true peak at or under −1.5 dBTP.
- Music under the voice is always lowered, and it fades out before the logo, not after.
- The voice is levelled once: never a second normalization.

## Names
One per line, as in `Miro Talen → MiroTalen`.
